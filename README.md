### Beyond Earth. Beyond Limits.

## Our Vision

SpaceY is an independent, community-driven initiative with the ambition to establish a real aerospace and space technology company.

We believe the future of space exploration should not be limited to governments and established corporations. Scientists, engineers, developers, researchers and communities around the world should have the opportunity to contribute to humanity's next frontier.

SpaceY begins as a global community with a long-term vision of becoming a scientific, engineering and technology enterprise.

## Our Mission

To develop accessible, intelligent and sustainable technologies that advance space exploration and benefit humanity on Earth and beyond.

## Research and Development

SpaceY aims to pursue research and development in the following areas:

- Artificial intelligence for space systems.
- Autonomous robotics.
- Satellite technology and communications.
- Orbital mechanics and mission simulation.
- Spacecraft digital twins.
- Earth observation and monitoring.
- Sustainable space infrastructure.
- Advanced engineering and materials research.

## The SpaceY Token

Token Name: SpaceY
Symbol: SPACEY

The holders of $SPACEY form the intended founding token-holder community of the SpaceY project.

Our vision is to build a project in which the community can participate in its development, contribute ideas, propose research initiatives and follow its scientific and technological progress.

Any future corporate ownership, governance rights or financial benefits must be established through formal legal arrangements. Token ownership does not automatically constitute ownership of company shares.

## SpaceY Technologies

Our long-term objective is to establish SpaceY Technologies as a legally registered company dedicated to scientific research, aerospace engineering and technological innovation.

The proposed company will focus on developing practical technologies, establishing research collaborations and progressing from software-based research toward physical engineering prototypes.

## First Research Prototype — Orbit Lab

A dependency-free Python model estimates ideal circular Earth orbit velocity and period using NASA/JPL gravitational parameters. It is educational software, not flight-qualified navigation.

```bash
python3 research/orbit.py --altitude-km 400
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
```

Four local automated tests passed on 27 November 2026. See the [research documentation](research/README.md) for assumptions and references, and the repository's Actions tab for remote test results.
