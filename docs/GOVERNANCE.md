# Community principles (draft)

The intended founding token-holder community consists of $SPACEY holders. Technical participation is open to non-holders as well. Token holding alone does not establish equity, dividend rights, corporate voting rights or entitlement to project assets.

Proposed process: submit an issue with purpose, scope, costs, risks, milestones and acceptance criteria; invite public discussion; record maintainer decisions and conflicts of interest; publish verifiable results. Community polling may be explored but is not binding corporate governance.

Before accepting funds or promising ownership, define the legal entity, jurisdiction, token rights, custody controls, reporting and applicable regulatory obligations with qualified counsel. Do not request seed phrases or private keys. Publish only verified official addresses and accounts.
