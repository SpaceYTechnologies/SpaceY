# SpaceY Technologies — formation proposal

**Status: proposed, not registered or operational.**

The objective is a research-led aerospace and technology enterprise. The first activity is software-based modelling and simulation, progressing to ground engineering only after technical and financial feasibility reviews.

## Formation gates

1. Confirm jurisdiction, name availability, trademark clearance and founders.
2. Establish a legal structure, accountable directors, intellectual property policy and compliant accounting.
3. Define token-holder participation and any separately documented ownership arrangements with qualified legal advisers.
4. Publish a costed research programme, funding sources, conflict-of-interest policy and measurable milestones.
5. Assess applicable aerospace licensing, export controls, spectrum rules and safety requirements before physical development or operations.

No corporate shares, assets, partnerships, facilities or flight capabilities are represented as existing. No purchase or contribution is required to read the public research.
